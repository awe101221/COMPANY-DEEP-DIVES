## What the price appears to embed (inference)

The stock is −8% YTD, −20% over 1 year, and −28% from the 52-week high ($2,548.50) while TTM revenue is +46% and TTM NI is −9% (Yahoo). That combination usually means the market has **cut the terminal-margin and/or duration assumption**, not the growth assumption.

A back-of-envelope reverse DCF (10% cost of equity, 3% terminal g, 10-year):

- If owner earnings start at ~$2.0bn (near TTM NI, generous vs FCF) and margins never recover, you need **~18–20% earnings CAGR for a decade** to justify $93.5bn. That is heroic if EBIT stays 7%.
- If EBIT margin recovers to 12% by year 5 on revenue growing 25% then fading to 8%, today’s price is closer to fair.
- If EBIT stays 7% and growth fades to mid-teens by year 5, the stock is **ahead of fundamentals**.

**I have not built the full model.** These are bounding inferences.
