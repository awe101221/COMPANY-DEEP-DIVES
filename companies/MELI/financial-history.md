## Reconciliation and quality notes

- **Revenue label:** Company reports “Net revenues and financial income,” combining commerce service/product revenue and fintech financial income — not pure product GMV take-rate revenue.  
- **Margin compression is intentional (management claim):** Q4’25 letter estimated strategic investments (BR free shipping, CBT, 1P, credit card) ≈ **5–6 ppt** of operating margin. Q2’26 OI −17% YoY while revenue +50%.  
- **Credit provisions** rose ~2 ppt of revenue YoY in Q2’26 as portfolio (+75%) outgrew revenue (+50%); management states credit revenue net of provisions was slightly higher as % of consolidated revenue vs Q2’25 (**management claim**).  
- **Adj. FCF** deducts credit book growth and customer-fund restricted cash increases — H1’26 adj. FCF $158M vs GAAP CFO $5.7B.  
- **Food delivery** included in GMV / items / unique buyers from Q2’25 — YoY comps partially affected through Q1’26.
