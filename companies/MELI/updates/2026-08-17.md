# MELI Intelligence Update — 2026-08-17

## 1. Bottom line

First probability-weighted bull / base / bear cases are live. MELI is growing revenue at a four-year-high pace while operating margin sits near 7% by design. Base case (45%) is continued compounding with only partial margin recovery; bull (25%) requires engagement investments to mature into 12%+ margins; bear (30%) is credit + competitive margin trap. Illustrative probability-weighted midpoint ~$2,140 vs ~$1,845 spot — modest expected upside, fat left tail. **Not a buy/sell recommendation.**

## 7. Deep-research finding

The Q2’26 letter’s strongest **decision-useful** (but not independently audited) claim is that ecosystemic users generate contribution profit that is “multiples” of marketplace-only + fintech-only users combined. If true and scalable, it rationalizes mid-single-digit consolidated margins as a temporary aggregation artifact. If overstated or competed away, the bear case dominates. This is the metric to demand more disclosure on.
