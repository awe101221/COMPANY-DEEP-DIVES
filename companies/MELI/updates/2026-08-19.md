## 6. Risks and disconfirming evidence

**Strongest case against “consignado is just a small launch”:**

- If the 70% CTPS conversion and 80% existing-client overlap are real, MELI can originate much faster than a four-day 5k pilot implies once the book “behaves.”
- Crédito do Trabalhador is a government distribution rail. Being late is a competitive gap vs banks that have been in since 2025; being early into an 8.6% NPL product is a credit gap.
- 10-K lending risk explicitly names **limited product history** and **interest-rate regulation** — both apply here (FGTS-guarantee and possible rate-cap talk have been in the Brazilian press).

**Strongest case against “Casas Bahia matters”:**

- MELI said the partnership is operating normally.
- Citi itself says the impact is marginal and that 1P could gain supplier share.
- CB’s own digital share is small on Citi’s numbers.

**Falsifiers:**

- Q3 letter or GS 2026-09-08: consignado originations or NIMAL commentary that implies a material book by year-end.
- Any 8-K, seller-communication leak, or letter language that the Casas Bahia style-out or fulfillment has broken bulky-category conversion.
- BCB next credit-statistics print: private-payroll NPL ≥10% with MELI still scaling.
