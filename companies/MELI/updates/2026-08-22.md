## 7. One deep-research finding

**Selected open question:** leftover of #9 — BCB payments-institution reserve *ratio*.

**Finding (full memo: `bcb-reserve-ratio-2026-08-22.md`):**

- Art. 22 is **100% of Brazil prepaid e-money + in-transit + pending credits**, in CCME cash or short Tesouro. Banks that issue e-money are cash-only (art. 23); Mercado Pago IP Ltda. is an IP, so the $202mn securities line is allowed.
- Res. 494/2025 is an authorization-calendar rewrite. It is not a ratio change.
- Do not apply the 21% bank sight-deposit *compulsório* to Mercado Pago wallets.
- Brazil ring-fence $11,376mn / consolidated funds payable $16,035mn = 70.9%. That fraction is **not** the statutory test.
- H1: BCB cash +$3,309mn, BCB securities −$584mn. They moved the book into cash. A cash-only rewrite would cost ~$202mn (3% of available cash).

**Working conclusion (75% confidence on the 100% reading):** the scarce assumption is still margin payback and credit aging, not a hidden fractional reserve. Wallet *growth* locking more cash into CCME is the live cash-flow fact (already in H1 adj. FCF).
