## 7. One deep-research finding

**Selected open question:** #4 — ads revenue and incremental margin in dollars.

**Finding (full memo: `ads-revenue-reconstruction-2026-08-17.md`):**

- Last reported take-rate: **Q4 2024 ads = 2.1% of GMV** ($306mn). Q4 2023 1.6% × 1.41 growth cross-checks that print.
- After Q4 2024 the company stopped printing ads/GMV and printed growth only. Q2 2026 ads **+73% USD / +62% FXN**; GMV **+44% USD** ⇒ take-rate **× ~1.20 YoY** (calculation).
- Base Q2 2026 ads **~$559mn** (range $483–634mn). TTM sketch **~$1.9bn**.
- Management “>10% LatAm digital-ad share” is consistent with an eMarketer-like ~$23bn digital market, not with $43–50bn industry reports. Do not invert the large market into a $4bn+ MELI ads number.
- Incremental margin is **not disclosed**. A 4% GMV outcome on today’s TTM GMV is ~+$1.1bn ads and, at an unverified 70–90% contribution analog, ~+2 EBIT ppts. Q2 PDA $1,276mn is still ~2× base ads.

**Working conclusion (55% confidence on the dollar range):** ads is a ~5% of revenue, mid-2% of GMV, 60%+ FXN growth line. It supports the *existence* of a high-margin offset. It does not yet support raising the probability that 2027 EBIT returns to double digits.
