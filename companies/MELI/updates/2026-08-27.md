## 7. Deep-research finding

Leftover of **#9** — `customer-funds-mx-cl-ar-2026-08-27.md`:

- **Mexico:** LRITF art. 46. Same-day 100% segregation into distinct sight accounts, 1-day govt/Banxico *reporto*, or an admin trust. Sight-deposit cap = max(1 million UDIS, 2× peak 24-hour redemption). MercadoLibre, S.A. de C.V., IFPE authorized 2022-05-11 (DOF).
- **Argentina:** BCRA TO 4.1.2. 100% in peso sight accounts at local banks, at all times. 4.2.2: not a bank deposit, no SEDESA on the CVU. Mercadolibre S.R.L. was on the 2020-12-31 PSPCP register (C89162). 2026 live certificate not pulled.
- **Chile:** BCCH III.J.1.3 II.B.vii segregation in cash or Annex 2 instruments, **plus** CMF NCG 541 reserve \(RL=\max(0.1C_m, ALp-Pp-Frp)\). Not a Brazil-style 100% cash-or-Tesouro test. Mercado Pago Emisora S.A. is the prepaid issuer.

Do not apply the 21% Brazilian bank *compulsório* to any of these. Do not write a Chile-style net-of-flows ratio onto Brazil, Mexico, or Argentina.
