## 6. Risks and disconfirming evidence

**Strongest case against treating this as material:**

- The cash cap is ~$1.2 million. Even if levied in full on Mercado Pago alone — which the gacetilla does not say — it does not move EPS.
- The action is **provincial consumer defense**, not BCRA prudential regulation. Buenos Aires cannot set Mercado Pago’s capital, reserve, or national rate rules.
- Six firms are named. This is an industry political event, not a MELI-specific 8-K fact.
- Management (Infobae, 2026-08-14) says the 1,375.94% CFTEA bucket is ~**2%** of loans and that mora is “in line with the main private banks” and “significantly below” non-bank averages. If true, Kicillof’s 24.5% wallet path is the *industry*, not MELI.
- The stock was up on Aug 20. No IR correction, no 8-K.

**Strongest case for watching it anyway:**

- Argentina is ~40% of disclosed direct contribution. A forced change in collection intensity, cooling-off (“botón de arrepentimiento”), or CFT presentation can hit originations or recoveries without a large fine.
- Official BCRA data already show the *industry* under stress: PNFC irregularity **26.9%** and Fintech **26.2%** in February 2026 (report published 2026-06-04). Bank household irregularity **11.2%**. Those prints pre-date this week; they make the political attack cheaper to launch.
- A pending criminal usury filing asks for a collection stay. No order yet; the request is the tail.

## 7. One deep-research finding

**Selected open question:** new #15 — size the 2026-08-20 Buenos Aires consumer-defense action and separate official industry mora from Mercado Pago’s undisclosed Argentina book. Full memo: `argentina-consumer-defense-2026-08-21.md`.

**Finding:**

1. **Official action (reported fact).** GBA gacetilla 2026-08-20: expediente after 2,240 H1 complaints against six wallets including Mercado Pago; descargos + rate/CFT information requests; statutory cap ARS 1,883 million plus reversal. Infobae adds the alleged counts: harassment of over-indebted households, unclear credit terms, missing cooling-off button. Those extra counts are **ministerial claims via the press**, not additional official-document line items.
2. **Cash is not the story (calculation).** ~$1.24 million at ARS 1,515 / USD. De minimis.
3. **Industry mora is real; MELI’s Argentina mora is not in the 10-Q (reported fact / gap).** BCRA PNFC report (data February 2026, published 4 June 2026): total irregularity 26.9%; Fintech 26.2% (+6.6 ppts vs August 2025); personal loans 34.1%; cards 19.4%; bank household irregularity 11.2%. Fintech average TNA 144% in February 2026. Kicillof’s speech numbers (wallets 5.7% → 24.5%; banks 2.5% → 12.8%) are **not a new official series**. They sit near, but do not match, those February prints and may use a later month (he cited May). Do not write 24.5% into MELI’s book.
4. **Management claim still unverified.** Infobae 2026-08-14: MP consumer TNA 48–249%, max CFTEA 1,375.94%; that bucket ~2% of loans; mora “in line with main private banks.” Group 10-Q 15–90 NPL is 7.0% and 90–360 DPD is 18.7% of *gross loans company-wide*. That is a different definition, geography, and window. It neither confirms nor kills the “in line with private banks” claim.

**Working conclusion (55% confidence):** this is a political/regulatory *watch item* in the high-leverage Argentina P&L, not a 2026 earnings event. The prior on industry mora is already poor. The missing number is Mercado Pago’s own Argentina irregularity.
