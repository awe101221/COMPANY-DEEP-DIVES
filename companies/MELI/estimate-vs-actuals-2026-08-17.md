# MELI eight-quarter estimate vs actuals

**As-of:** 2026-08-17 01:30 UTC  
**Question:** Does the Q3 2024–Q2 2026 print history support treating street FY27 EPS **$55.99** as a reliable rebound, or is that a recency / mean-reversion assumption?  
**Status:** Progressed. Not a terminal LSEG/FactSet extract.

## Method

- **Actuals** are GAAP from the earnings 8-K EX-99.1 income statement or cover letter. Diluted EPS is the figure the 8-K labels “Diluted net income available to shareholders per common share.”
- **Earnings consensus** prefers contemporaneous **LSEG net-income polls quoted by Reuters** on print day. Yahoo Finance “Earnings History” EPS is the secondary series (same vendor as the current FY26/FY27 consensus). Benzinga is used only where Reuters/Yahoo do not publish a number, and is labeled.
- **Revenue consensus** prefers Reuters/LSEG on print day; Benzinga is the fallback for Q3–Q4 2024 and Q2 2025.
- Vendor EPS prints are **not interchangeable**. Where they disagree, the table shows a range and the call (beat/miss) is taken from LSEG NI when Reuters published one.
- Share count is stable (~50.70mn). LSEG NI can be translated to an implied EPS for comparison; that translation is a **calculation**, not a published EPS consensus.

## What the errors say

**Revenue: 8/8 beats or in-line.** The smallest beat is Q3’24 (~1%). From Q1’25 onward the beat is 3–8% when a Reuters/LSEG number exists. Street systematically under-forecasts the conversion of GMV/TPV into reported revenue. That bias survived the 2025–26 investment cycle.

**Earnings: two regimes.**

1. **Pre-threshold (Q3’24–Q1’25).** Q3’24 was a large NI miss (LSEG $542mn vs $397mn; Bloomberg’s contemporaneous poll was $513mn — still a miss). Street then *over-cut* Q4’24 (LSEG NI $401.5mn / EBIT $612mn vs $639mn / $820mn). Q1’25 was a clean double beat as Argentina recovered.
2. **Investment cycle (Q2’25–Q2’26).** Brazil free-shipping threshold cut in June 2025. The next four prints were **revenue beats with NI misses or in-lines** (Q2’25, Q3’25, Q4’25, Q1’26). Q2’26 finally beat LSEG NI ($466mn vs $433mn) and Yahoo EPS ($9.19 vs $8.89), but EBIT margin 6.7% was still the quality miss that sold the stock.

The mean is pulled up by the Q4’24 +59% beat. **The median of the last four LSEG-numbered prints is a miss.** That is the relevant window for judging whether street “has the 2026–27 P&L.”

**Yahoo EPS history (last four, retrieved 2026-08-17):** Q3’25 −10.5%, Q4’25 −3.6%, Q1’26 +0.4%, Q2’26 +3.4%. Same shape: misses into the investment, then a small beat once street had already cut FY26.

## Implication for FY27 EPS $55.99

Street FY26/FY27 (Yahoo, unchanged vs 2026-08-16): **$38.31 / $55.99**. FY26 is −0.4% vs FY25 $39.40. FY27 is **+46%**. That jump is a *margin* forecast, not a growth forecast. Yahoo’s own FY26/FY27 revenue path is $41.63bn → $53.12bn (+28%).

If FY27 NI is $55.99 × 50.697mn = **$2.84bn** (calculation) on $53.12bn revenue, NI margin is **5.3%**. Backing into EBIT at a 28% tax rate and ~$0.2bn other-expense drag (same method as the 2026-08-16 reverse DCF) still implies **~7.8% EBIT** — the number already in `expectations-map.md`. The 2027 *multiple* (32.9× on the 2026-08-14 close), not 2027 consensus EBIT, is what embeds the later recovery to 12–13%.

**Working conclusion (60% confidence):** the eight-quarter tape does **not** support treating $56 of 2027 EPS as a high-confidence rebound. Street is good at being too low on revenue and, once an investment cycle starts, too high on near-term earnings. It has now cut 2026 EPS (Yahoo −4.0% / −7.5% over 30/90 days). It has **not** abandoned the 2027 shape. That is consistent with the existing variant: the tape is a recovery tape; the P&L is not yet a recovery P&L.

**Strongest counterargument:** Q2’26 was a genuine LSEG NI beat, and Yahoo’s last two EPS prints were in-line to a beat. If that is the start of street catching the run-rate, FY27 $56 becomes a base case rather than a bull case. Falsifier: two more quarters of EPS beats ≥5% with EBIT margin ≥8% would make this memo stale. Opposite falsifier: Q3’26 EBIT ≤5.5% with another NI miss would confirm the investment-cycle error regime is still on.

- Q4’24 Benzinga EPS est. $7.56 is directionally consistent with LSEG NI $401.5mn / 50.70mn ≈ **$7.92** (calculation). Tradersunion’s $10.21 is an outlier; discarded.
