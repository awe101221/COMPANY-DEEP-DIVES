# MercadoLibre, Inc. (Nasdaq: MELI)

This directory is the authoritative knowledge base for MercadoLibre research.

## Scope

All MELI agents and automations must read and write only within `companies/MELI/` unless the user explicitly authorizes a repository-wide change. Never modify another company's directory.

## Knowledge-base files

- `company-dossier.md`: durable company facts, history, management, businesses, geography, competition, and regulation.
- `financial-history.md`: sourced historical financial statements, operating metrics, reconciliations, and definition notes.
- `kpi-dictionary.md`: precise definitions and disclosure changes for every material MELI KPI.
- `thesis-ledger.md`: dated bull, base, and bear cases, probabilities, valuation assumptions, evidence, and falsifiers.
- `risk-register.md`: probability-weighted risks, transmission mechanisms, leading indicators, and mitigants.
- `source-ledger.md`: source provenance for material facts and calculations.
- `open-questions.md`: unresolved research questions ranked by decision impact.
- `updates/`: dated incremental intelligence reports named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- `changelog.md`: dated knowledge-base change log.
- `expectations-map.md`: what the price and consensus appear to embed.
- `quarterly-tracker.md`: print-by-print expectations vs actuals.
- `catalyst-calendar.md`: dated events that can move the thesis.
- `country-dossiers.md`: Brazil, Mexico, Argentina operating + macro transmission.
- `reverse-dcf-2026-08-16.md`: first completed reverse DCF / EV bridge / SOTP.
- `estimate-vs-actuals-2026-08-17.md`: eight-quarter (Q3’24–Q2’26) estimate-vs-actuals reconstruction.
- `ads-revenue-reconstruction-2026-08-17.md`: ads dollars reconstructed from letter take-rates and growth rates.
- `argentina-consumer-defense-2026-08-21.md`: 2026-08-20 BA Province expediente + BCRA PNFC industry mora vs MELI’s undisclosed Argentina book.

## Operating rules

- Browse and verify current information before recording it.
- Prefer primary sources and cite every material claim.
- Include an explicit as-of date for time-sensitive information.
- Separate reported facts, management statements, third-party estimates, calculations, and inferences.
- Preserve prior conclusions and record why a thesis or assumption changed.
- Do not manufacture an update when nothing material changed.
