# MELI Company Dossier

**Company:** MercadoLibre, Inc.  
**Primary listing:** Nasdaq: MELI  
**Knowledge-base status:** Initial primary-source baseline  
**Last verified:** 2026-08-17

## Corporate history

- Founded 1999; Nasdaq IPO 2007.  
- Self-described largest e-commerce and fintech ecosystem in Latin America (company materials).  
- **Reported fact (10-K FY2025):** E-commerce platform in 18 countries; Mercado Pago in 8 (Argentina, Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Uruguay, Ecuador). Venezuela deconsolidated since Dec 2017.  
- Marketplace leader in region by GMV; Mercado Pago leader in MAUs among fintechs in Argentina, Chile, Mexico and #2 in Brazil (**company claim in 10-K**).

## Leadership and governance

- **2026-01-01:** Ariel Szarfsztejn became CEO; founder Marcos Galperin became Executive Chairman (Q4’25 shareholder letter).  
- CFO: Martín de los Santos (signatory on Q2’26 8-K).  
- Delaware corporation; principal offices Montevideo, Uruguay (10-K).

## Business and ecosystem map

| Pillar | What it is | Monetization (high level) |
|---|---|---|
| Commerce / Marketplace | Marketplace + logistics (Mercado Envíos) + 1P + CBT + MELI+ loyalty | Fees, shipping, 1P product sales, subscriptions, ads, classifieds |
| Mercado Ads | On-platform and expanding video/brand inventory | Ad net revenue (high incremental margin) |
| Mercado Pago – payments/acquiring | Wallet, QR, online checkout, POS/MPOS | Take rates on off-platform and acquiring TPV; device sales |
| Mercado Pago – AUM / banking-like | Yielding account, savings, investments | Net interest / spread after pass-through (esp. Brazil) |
| Mercado Crédito | Consumer, merchant, credit card | Interest and fees; NIMAL after provisions & funding |
| Insurtech / other | Insurance and adjacent | Fees / premiums (smaller) |

**Strategic investment themes (2025–26 letters):** lower Brazil free-shipping threshold; credit-card issuance; 1P selection; China–LatAm CBT / China FC; MELI+; AI search/seller tools.

## Competitive environment

From FY2025 10-K (reported / company risk disclosure):

- **Commerce:** Intense competition from local e-commerce players and international platforms, especially in Brazil and Mexico; also omnichannel retailers.  
- **Fintech:** Banks, traditional payment methods, and local/global fintechs (credit, cards, savings, AUM).  
- **Ads:** Large global digital platforms dominate LatAm digital advertising.  

Specific competitor market-share time series not yet compiled in this KB (open question).

## Regulation and external exposures

- Multi-jurisdiction payments, credit, consumer, tax, and labor regimes.  
- Mexico tax reform cited as demand headwind in 2026 letters.  
- Argentina currency, inflation, and controls historically material for translation and local economics.  
- Customer funds subject to regulatory restrictions (large restricted cash / funds payable balances).

## Capital allocation

- Priority: reinvest in logistics, free shipping, credit book, 1P/CBT, product/AI.  
- FY2025 adj. FCF $1.5B after $1.3B capex and >$6.5B credit portfolio investment (partially offset by ~$2.4B fintech funding) — Q4’25 letter.  
- H1’26: capex $712M; Δ loans receivable $(4.1)B; adj. FCF $158M; company net debt rose to $6.4B.  
- Minimal buybacks; no meaningful dividend.
