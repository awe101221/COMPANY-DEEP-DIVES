# Argentina consumer-defense expediente and industry mora

**As-of:** 2026-08-21.  
**Status:** Incremental memo. Does not replace the Argentina country dossier or the group credit deep-dive on other branches.  
**Question:** Does the 2026-08-20 Buenos Aires Province investigation of Mercado Pago change MELI’s cash, credit, or Argentina direct-contribution risk?

---

## 1. Official action (reported fact)

On Thursday 20 August 2026 the governor of Buenos Aires Province, Axel Kicillof, and production minister Augusto Costa announced a consumer-defense investigation of virtual wallets.

Primary source: [GBA gacetilla](https://gba.gob.ar/comunicacion_publica/gacetillas/kicillof_y_costa_anunciaron_medidas_para_defender_los_consumidores), dated “Jueves 20 de Agosto 2026”.

What the gacetilla itself states:

- The Provincial Directorate of Consumer Defense received **2,240 complaints in H1 2026** against **Mercado Pago, Naranja Digital, Ualá, Personal Pay, Brubank, and Cencosud** for alleged breaches of consumer-defense rules, harassment, and “hostigamiento.”
- An **expediente de investigación** is open. The firms are being asked to detail **how they set interest rates** and **the composition of Costo Financiero Total (CFT)**.
- The firms must present **descargos**.
- If breaches are proven, the statute provides for fines of **up to ARS 1,883 million** and an obligation to reverse non-compliant practices.

What the gacetilla does **not** state:

- Mercado Pago’s share of the 2,240 complaints.
- A finding of liability.
- A five-day deadline (a secondary article used ARS 1,815 million and a five-day clock; **rejected**).
- A BCRA or national prudential measure.
- Any Mercado Pago Argentina NPL, book, or yield number.

Infobae (2026-08-20, 18:25 EST) and La Nación (2026-08-20) reprint the same official quotes and add ministerial color: alleged harassment of over-indebted families, unclear credit terms, missing “botón de arrepentimiento,” automatic acceptance of new T&Cs, reversed burden on account-security incidents, and cross-contract set-off. Treat those extra counts as **ministerial claims via the press** unless a filed expediente becomes public.

**Timing vs last run:** PR #8’s as-of was ~14:00 UTC on 2026-08-20 (11:00 Argentina). The press conference was Thursday afternoon; Infobae’s stamp is 18:25 EST. This event is **after** that cutoff. PR #8 had already recorded the 2026-08-14 Infobae rate article and the 2026-08-19 Solano usury filing.

### 3.2 Kicillof’s speech numbers (political claim)

Infobae’s account of the 2026-08-20 conference: over “the last two and a half years,” bank mora 2.5% → **12.8%**; wallets 5.7% → **24.5%**; non-bank financieras 17.5% → **43%** (the last defined as >3 months late). He also said 60% of adults are indebted (21 million) and 20% / 6 million are in mora.

These figures are **not in the GBA gacetilla**. They sit near the BCRA February prints (fintech 26.2%, bank households 11.2%) but do not match them. He referred to a May peak. Until a later official series is published, treat 24.5% as a **governor’s claim**, directionally consistent with the February industry prior, **not as Mercado Pago NPL**.

### 3.3 Management claim (Infobae, 2026-08-14)

Mercado Pago, via Infobae:

- Consumer / personal-loan TNA **48–249%**; corresponding TEA 60.12–863.48%; CFTEA **76.36–1,375.94%**.
- Card compensatory TNA **84.11%** (TEA 125.54%, CFTEA 165.76%).
- The 1,375.94% CFTEA bucket is ~**2%** of loans and applies to the smallest lines / least-known users.
- Mora “in line with the main private banks” for individuals, and “significantly below” the non-bank average.
- Own scoring on >3,000 variables.

**Epistemic status:** management claims through a newspaper. The TNA/CFTEA schedule is consistent with Mercado Pago’s own help-page screenshots as described in that article; the 2% mix and the bank-comparable mora are **unverified**.

### 3.4 What MELI’s 10-Q actually shows (group, not Argentina)

Q2 2026 10-Q Note 4, as already loaded on `main`:

- Gross loans $16,375 million; 15–90 NPL **7.0%**; 90–360 DPD **18.7%** of gross loans.
- Write-off at **360 days**. 15–90 is a short window; 90–360 stays on book.
- No Argentina product cut. No Mercado Pago Argentina irregularity.

These group ratios **cannot** be compared one-for-one with BCRA “irregularidad” (typically 90+ on the local definition) or with Kicillof’s 24.5%. A 18.7% 90–360 stock on a 75% growing book is already the bear-case credit fact at *group* level. It does not tell us whether Argentina is better or worse than the 26% fintech industry print.

## 5. Investment transmission

Argentina is **18% of Q2 revenue and ~40% of disclosed direct contribution** (Q2 letter). High local DC means a consumption, FX, or credit shock hits group EBIT harder than the revenue mix suggests. That was already in the thesis.

**This week adds:**

1. A **named provincial expediente** — cheap politically, small in cash, real as a conduct-risk flag.
2. Confirmation that the **official industry prior** (BCRA Fintech 26.2% irregularity, 144% average TNA) is ugly enough that an opposition governor can open a file without inventing the backdrop.
3. No new information on **Mercado Pago’s own** Argentina mora, which management says looks like a private bank and which the 10-Q does not break out.

**12-month paths (subjective):**

| Path | Rough P | MELI impact |
|---|---:|---|
| Fine only / T&C edits / no book change | 55% | De minimis. Noise. |
| Collection, cooling-off, or CFT-presentation changes that slow AR originations or recoveries | 25% | Hits AR DC at the margin; not a group-thesis break unless originations were carrying the 40% DC share. |
| National / BCRA rate-cap or a court collection stay | 15% | Could matter. Would be a new event. |
| Nothing, and next BCRA print shows fintech irregularity rolling over | 5% | Mildly supportive of the “MP is the clean fintech” claim — still unproven. |

These probabilities are for *this political/regulatory node*, not a rewrite of the 25/45/30 thesis set.

## 6. What would change my mind

**Falsify “de minimis”:** a published descargo that admits a large complaint share; a BCRA or Nación circular on wallet CFTs; a collection-stay order; or a Q3 letter that discloses Argentina consumer NPL near the 26% industry print.

**Falsify “industry mora is the right prior”:** a primary Mercado Pago or 10-Q Argentina cut that is in line with private-bank ~11% household irregularity *on a comparable 90+ definition*.

**Do not treat as proof of either:** Kicillof’s 24.5%, Infobae’s 2% high-CFTEA mix, or the group 7.0% 15–90 NPL.
