**As-of:** 2026-08-18. Price as-of 2026-08-17 close **$1,787.57** (Yahoo; −3.09% vs 2026-08-14 $1,844.58). FY26/FY27 EPS consensus unchanged vs 2026-08-17.

## What the price appears to embed (inference, now reverse-DCF backed)

The stock is ~−8% YTD and ~−22% over 52 weeks (Yahoo) while TTM revenue is +46% and TTM NI is −9%. That combination usually means the market has **cut the near-term margin assumption**, not the growth assumption.

The completed reverse DCF (`reverse-dcf-2026-08-16.md`) was built on the $1,844.58 close / $99.9bn EV. The 2026-08-17 close is 3% lower; the DCF was **not** rebuilt (below the 10% redo rule). That $99.9bn EV was consistent with:

- **~13% terminal EBIT at ~9% WACC and improving cash conversion**, or
- **~12% terminal EBIT at 10% WACC and a faster revenue path (~$175bn by 2035)**, or
- **~16–18% terminal EBIT at 10% WACC on the street-fade path** — above any sourced historical year.

It is **not** consistent with 6.7% EBIT as mid-cycle (that path is ~$720/share at 10% WACC). At $1,788 the stock is still far above that path.

Street near-term is a trough: FY26 EPS $38.31 vs FY25 $39.40; implied EBIT margins ~7.0% / ~7.8% in 2026–27 (my calculation). The recovery lives in the **32.9× 2027E** multiple, not in 2026–27 consensus EBIT.

**Eight-quarter test (2026-08-17):** street FY27 EPS $55.99 fails as a high-confidence rebound. Revenue beat or in-line 8/8 from Q3’24–Q2’26; LSEG NI missed the first three investment-cycle prints (Q2–Q4’25) and only beat in Q2’26 after the FY26 cut. See `estimate-vs-actuals-2026-08-17.md`. This is an expectations finding, not a new operating fact.

**Ads test (2026-08-17):** street does not publish an auditable ads line. Independent reconstruction puts Q2’26 ads at ~$0.5–0.6bn (~5.5% of revenue, mid-2% of GMV) and TTM ~$2bn. That is not large enough to be the 2027 margin-recovery story on its own. See `ads-revenue-reconstruction-2026-08-17.md`.
