## Interpretation rules

- Never treat GMV, TPV, revenue, credit originations, credit portfolio balances, or cash as interchangeable.
- Reconcile changes in definitions before comparing periods.
- Label reported, calculated, and estimated values explicitly.
- Customer funds and restricted cash are not corporate free cash.
