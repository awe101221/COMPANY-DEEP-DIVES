## Source priority

1. SEC filings and MercadoLibre investor-relations materials.
2. Official regulatory, court, tax, statistical, and central-bank sources.
3. Direct management interviews and complete earnings calls.
4. Reputable industry data and competitor disclosures.
5. High-quality financial and local-language journalism.
6. Alternative data and expert commentary.
7. Social media or anonymous claims only as research leads.
