# MELI ownership reconstruction — 2026-08-18

**Question:** Open question #11 — insider / 13D ownership after the June 2026 filings.  
**As-of:** 2026-08-18. No Form 4, Form 3, 13D, or 13G after 2026-06-26.  
**Status:** Progressed, not closed. 13F lag remains (latest 13Gs are 3/31/2026 event dates).

## Bottom line

The June 18, 2026 Schedule 13D/A is an **estate-planning restructure, not an open-market sale**. Meliga No. 1 LP distributed **3,400,136** MELI shares to Meliga No. 1 Corp. for **no consideration**. The Galperin Trust group still reports **6.70%** beneficial ownership with **shared** (not sole) voting and dispositive power, and **no Item 4 control plans**.

That is **not** a founder control block. Combined with the April 2026 proxy, it shows:

1. Economic founder exposure sits in an irrevocable South Dakota trust, not in Marcos Galperin’s personal name (he reports **35** shares).
2. Named executive officers own almost no stock. Alignment is the **cash LTRP**, already in the dossier.
3. The only >5% holders on file are the Galperin group (13D), Baillie Gifford (passive 13G, **6.38%**), and Capital Research Global Investors (new 13G, **5.4%**).
4. No Section 16 open-market **sale** appears after Q2. The last open-market insider prints are **buys** (Aguzin in May; Melamud in June) plus routine director grants and a Tolda gift.

The 13D count is **150,000 shares below** the April 14, 2026 proxy figure, which itself cited the November 1, 2024 13D/A. Amendment No. 2 does **not** describe that reduction. Mechanism is an open residual (sale, gift, or earlier estate step). Do not label it “founder selling” without a transaction record.

### Galperin group — what Amendment No. 2 actually did

Read from the [primary 13D/A text](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1099590/000143774926021169/0001437749-26-021169.txt):

- Effective **2026-06-17**, Meliga No. 1 LP (New Zealand) distributed **(i)** 3,400,136 MELI shares to Meliga No. 1 Corp. (BVI), **(ii)** $1,000,000 cash to Meliga Corp., and **(iii)** $57,664.41 cash to Volorama Stichting (Dutch GP).
- **“No pecuniary or other consideration was exchanged in connection with the transfer.”**
- Purpose: “transfer certain assets from Meliga No. 1 LP to its parent entities for no consideration, as part of an ongoing estate-planning restructuring process.”
- After the distribution, Meliga LP and Volorama **no longer** beneficially own ≥5%.
- Galperin Trust /SD is an **irrevocable** South Dakota trust that **owns 100% of Meliga Corp.** Shares “may not be voted or disposed of without the approval of Galperin Trust /SD.”
- Dispositions / reinvestment require an **Investment Advisor** plus **unanimous consent of two Protectors**. Distributions to beneficiaries require a **Distribution Advisor** plus the same two Protectors.
- **“The Reporting Persons have no current intention, plan or proposal with respect to items 4(a) through (j)”** — no bid, no going-private, no board change, no extraordinary transaction disclosed.
- No other transactions in the prior 60 days besides the Distribution.

## 4. What this does *not* say

- It does **not** restore founder “skin in the game” in the personal-ownership sense. The 6.70% is in an irrevocable trust with an investment advisor and two protectors.
- It does **not** create a control overhang. Charter already has a **20% voting-power limitation** (2025 10-K, already in the dossier). The 13D group is one-third of that cap.
- It does **not** explain the 150,000-share 13D decline versus the April proxy.
- It is **not** a 13F reconstruction. CRGI / Baillie are 3/31 prints. Q2 13Fs were not pulled.
- Sean Summers becoming ads EVP is a **governance/org fact**, not a new ads-revenue print.
